Overview / 项目简介

Benchmark Radar is a living database and search engine for AI benchmarks and evaluation. It connects benchmark discovery with datasets, code, reported scores, and source evidence.

My merged open-source contributions include an agent-benchmark digest connector, preservation of source evidence across paper revisions, recovery of cited benchmark release dates, and fixes that distinguish unreported adoption counters from measured zero.

Benchmark Radar 是持续更新的 AI 评测基准数据库与搜索引擎。我参与了智能体评测信息源接入、论文修订时的证据保留、基准发布日期补全，以及缺失使用量指标的处理。
